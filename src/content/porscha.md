## Hello

I'm Porscha. I make things — mostly software, sometimes art, occasionally
things that are hard to file under either.

This site is my workshop. It's where finished work sits next to half-built
experiments, because that's how a real workbench looks.

## I build things

Right now that means small, personal tools: a capture app that remembers
*where* you were when an idea hit, a way of looking at your own data that
doesn't feel like a quarterly report, a habit companion that forgives you.

I like software that feels like it was made by a person, for a person.

## How I got here

I started by taking things apart — radios, websites, other people's code —
and eventually noticed I enjoyed the putting-back-together more. A few
false starts taught me that shipping something small and honest beats
polishing something imaginary. cloakli, my first real app, never found its
feet. Everything since is built on what it taught me.

## Things I care about

- Tools that respect attention instead of farming it.
- Work-in-progress shown honestly, not staged.
- Software that stays understandable a year later.
- Making things by hand, even when a generator would be faster.

## Selected history
