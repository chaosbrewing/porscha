## Experiment

Thirty days. One reading per day — temperature, noise, light, whatever —
written in a pocket notebook. No automation allowed.

## Result

Confirmed, unexpectedly. Hand-logging made me pick readings that meant
something ("kitchen at 7am, before anyone's up") instead of readings that
were easy. PRISM's reflection views inherit this directly: the views are
organized around moments, not sensors.

## Notes

Automation is a decision about what's worth noticing, made once, in a
hurry, forever. Paper renegotiates it daily.
