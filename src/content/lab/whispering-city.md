## Experiment

A small text engine where every location accumulates a memory of what
players have done there, and leaks fragments of it back to later visitors
as environmental whispers.

## Result

Still running. The memory model works; the hard part is making the
whispers feel uncanny instead of random. Current approach: whispers only
surface details the *current* player could plausibly almost-know.

## Notes

The engine wants to become a library. Resisting until the fiction is good.
