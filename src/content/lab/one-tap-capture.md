## Experiment

A bare prototype: one giant button, voice or text, no categories, no
titles. Everything sorted later by time and place.

## Result

Half-confirmed. Capture volume tripled. Triage stayed manageable — but
only because place-grouping made review feel like revisiting rather than
sorting. Without the location index, the pile would have been a landfill.

## Notes

This experiment is why Kubli exists.
