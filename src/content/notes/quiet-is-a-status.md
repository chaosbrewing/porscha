---
title: Quiet is a status, not a failure
date: 2026-06-14
kind: observation
project: habi
---

habi has been quiet for a while, and I kept feeling like I owed it an
apology — or worse, a fake progress update.

Then I added "Quiet" as a first-class project status on this site, right
next to "Building" and "Shipped." The moment it had a name, the guilt
evaporated. A workshop is full of resting projects. That's what the
shelves are for.
