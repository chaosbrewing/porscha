why: >
  Ideas arrive in places — on a walk, in a hardware store aisle, halfway up

  undifferentiated inbox, and place is exactly the handle memory wants.
lessons:
  - Capture has to be faster than forgetting. Anything over two taps loses.
  - Location is a filing system nobody has to maintain.
  - An offline queue is not an edge case; it is the primary case.

Kubli is a place-based capture tool. You stash an idea, a link, a photo, a
fragment — and Kubli quietly remembers where you were. Later, the pile
re-sorts itself around the places of your life, which turns out to be how
you actually remember things.

The current work is entirely about the capture flow: making the moment of
having an idea and the moment of saving it feel like the same moment.

It grew out of cloakli, an earlier private-capture experiment that never
quite worked. Kubli keeps its lesson — capture is sacred, everything else
is optional — and discards the rest.
