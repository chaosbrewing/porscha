---
project: prism
why: >
  Personal data mostly gets collected, rarely gets looked at. PRISM exists
  to make the looking worthwhile — views you'd open on a Sunday morning out
  of curiosity, not obligation.
lessons:
  - A chart you don't want to look at is just surveillance with extra steps.
  - Time is the only axis everyone already understands.
  - Export early. Data you can't take with you isn't yours.
screenshots: []
---

PRISM takes the scattered records a life produces and turns them into
something you can actually sit with — timelines, densities, quiet
patterns. Less dashboard, more reflection.

The current milestone is the first set of views that earn their place: a
timeline that reads like a diary you didn't have to write, and a density
view that shows the shape of a season at a glance.
