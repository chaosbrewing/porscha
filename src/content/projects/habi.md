why: >
  Every habit app is built for the streak. habi is built for the return —
  because missing days is not failure, it's the normal texture of a life.
lessons:
  - Guilt is a terrible retention strategy.
  - The smallest possible check-in is the right size.
screenshots: []

habi is a small, forgiving habit companion. It assumes you will miss days
and designs for the moment you come back, instead of punishing you for
having left.

It's resting at the moment — intentionally. The core loop works, and the
next round of work is waiting for Kubli's capture flow to ship first.
